Our Story

Named for the wheel that never stops turning.

BLACKWHEELCAKE started with one cast-iron mixer wheel and a baker who refused to bake anything she wouldn't eat herself. The name stuck — it's the wheel that turns the batter, and the cycle that repeats every single morning before sunrise.

Today the wheel is bigger, the ovens are hotter, but the standard hasn't moved: small batches, real butter, nothing that sits more than a day before it's sold.

How It's Made

One Wheel, Four Turns

Every product on the menu passes through the same four stages before it reaches the counter.

01

Mix & Ferment

Flour, butter, and time — doughs rest as long as they need to, not as long as the clock allows.

02

Shape & Proof

Hand-shaped in small batches, proofed slow at room temperature for real structure and flavor.

03

Bake & Cool

Stone-deck ovens, watched not timed. Cakes cool fully before they're ever filled or iced.

04

Finish & Serve

Glazed, dusted, or left plain — whatever the recipe actually calls for, nothing extra.
